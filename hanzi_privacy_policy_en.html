1. Introduction

Welcome to Hanzi Puzzle (the “App,” “we,” or “us”), an offline puzzle game independently developed and operated around decomposing Chinese characters into strokes.

We value your privacy and handle information generated through the App in accordance with applicable privacy laws and the rules of the app store through which you obtained the App.

This Policy explains what information the App processes, why it is processed, which third-party services are used, your rights, and how information is protected. Please read it carefully before using the App. By downloading, installing, launching, or continuing to use the App, you acknowledge this Policy.

2. Information We Process

2.1 Information we do not collect

No account is required, and the App does not upload the following information to any server operated by us:

  • Name, gender, date of birth, government ID number, phone number, or email address;
  • Unique device identifiers such as IDFA or IDFV, MAC address, IMEI, or serial number;
  • GPS location or IP address, except for minimal system-level communication required by Apple for purchases or iCloud;
  • Contacts, messages, call history, calendar, photos, video, microphone recordings, or camera images;
  • Identifiable information from third-party accounts, including social accounts or your Apple ID.

2.2 Information stored or processed locally

The following data remains only on your device or in the iCloud associated with your Apple ID and is not readable by us:

4.1 Apple services

StoreKit, iCloud, and Keychain are iOS system services. Apple may process information associated with your Apple ID while providing them. Apple controls that processing, and the App developer cannot access it. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

4.2 Google Mobile Ads SDK

AdMob is reserved in the App's dependency list but is not initialized or called in the current version, so it does not currently collect or transmit data. If enabled later, it may process IDFA only after ATT consent, device model, system version, approximate region, and ad interactions. We will update this Policy before enabling it. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK disclosure.

5. Storage, Sharing, and Transfer

5.1 Location and retention

The data described in Section 2.2 is stored on your device or in your Apple ID's iCloud. We operate no user-data server. Device data remains until you delete the App, clear the relevant iCloud data, or erase your device. Some Keychain data may remain after uninstalling due to iOS behavior.

5.2 Sharing and disclosure

We do not actively share, sell, or transfer your personal information. Disclosure would occur only with your explicit consent, when required by law or binding governmental or judicial process, or when necessary to protect the lawful rights of the App, users, or the public.

6. Data Security

  • Coin balance and progress are protected by the iOS Keychain and system encryption.
  • The App follows data-minimization and does not request unrelated permissions.
  • The App does not send personal information to a developer-operated server.
  • Purchases and iCloud synchronization use Apple's system services and security controls.

No system is absolutely secure. If a personal-data incident occurs, we will comply with applicable notification and reporting requirements.

7. Your Rights

7.1 Access

You can view your coin balance, level progress, and preferences inside the App. The App maintains no additional hidden user profile.

7.2 Deletion

  • Uninstall the App to remove its Application Support files and UserDefaults. Keychain entries may remain after uninstalling.
  • Delete iCloud data through iPhone Settings → your Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Hanzi Puzzle → Delete Data.
  • Erase the device to remove all information stored on it.

7.3 Withdrawal and complaints

You may stop using the App and delete its data to withdraw consent. Withdrawal does not affect prior lawful processing. For privacy questions or complaints, contact us using Section 10.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is an all-ages puzzle game suitable for children. It does not collect information that identifies children and contains no social, chat, or user-generated-content features. Because it offers coin-pack purchases, parents should consider enabling iOS Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → Purchases. A guardian who identifies an unauthorized use or purchase may contact us for assistance.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted in the same location with a new version and effective date. We will provide a prominent in-app notice for material changes, such as broader data collection, a new purpose, or a newly enabled third-party SDK.

Frequently asked questions

How do I start coloring?

On the home screen, pick a theme — Cute Animals, Dino World, Ocean Friends and so on — then choose a picture to open the coloring screen. The tools on the right switch between Fill, Marker, Pencil, Brush, Spray, Crayon and Pattern; the strip in the middle is where you pick a color or a pattern.

Is my progress saved when I leave?

Yes. Progress saves by itself, on the device. Open the same picture again and you carry on where you left off. Uninstalling the app removes the progress and artwork held in its local storage.

How do I undo or start over?

Use the undo button on the coloring screen to step back. Clear wipes the current picture so you can start again.

Why can't I save to Photos?
  1. Check that you allowed Budou to add photos when asked.
  2. If you declined earlier, open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, find Budou, and allow adding photos.
  3. Make sure the device has enough free storage, then try saving again.

Budou only asks for add-only access. It cannot read or scan any other photos in your library.

Why is there no music or sound?
  1. Tap the settings button at the top right of the home screen and check that Background music and Sound effects are switched on.
  2. Check the device volume, and that it isn't connected to another Bluetooth audio device.
  3. If another app is playing audio, the system may give that app priority.

Background music and sound effects can be turned on and off separately.

How do I delete artwork?

Delete a single piece from My Artwork, or clear everything from the app's Settings. Copies you already saved to Photos have to be deleted separately in the Photos app.

Does Budou need internet or an account?

No to both. Coloring works fully offline, and there is no sign-up or sign-in. Artwork, progress and preferences stay on the device by default.
